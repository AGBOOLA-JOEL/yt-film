# Code of conduct

This is a small, friendly project. In issues, discussions and comments:
- be respectful and patient; assume good intent;
- keep to the topic, and be specific (what you did, what happened, what you expected);
- no harassment, insults, discrimination, spam or self-promotion unrelated to the project;
- don't share other people's private information or copyrighted material you don't have the right to share.

The maintainer may edit, hide or lock comments, close issues and block accounts that don't follow this.

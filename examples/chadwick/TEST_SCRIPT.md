# Carnegie's Daughter: the effects and illustration test

Shows every animation style for the new films: particle smoke and breath, flames with real light, falling snow,
objects lifted out of the picture (the note slipping from the muff and landing), handwriting on the note, an acting
face, a proper drawing made on the picture (ink, then colour), the con explained as drawings on a card, a rolling
money counter, candlelight and fire, a glass-shatter transition, a crowd, a stamp, the note torn in two, a footnote.

---

## T01 the carriage
New York, around 1902. A lawyer named Dillon waits in a carriage on Fifth Avenue, checking his watch. The woman he drove here has been inside Andrew Carnegie's mansion for nearly half an hour.

## T02 the note
When she finally comes out, she stumbles on the carriage step, and a folded paper slips from her muff. Dillon picks it up. I promise to pay... two million dollars. Andrew Carnegie.

## T03 the trick
It was a forgery. And it worked like this. The note went into a sealed envelope. The envelope went into a bank vault. And the bank's receipt went to the next banker, who lent her money on it.

## T04 the queen
By 1904, she had borrowed about two million dollars, and she spent it like a queen.

## T05 the fall
Then one banker asked for his money back. Crowds came to the bank doors. And when the envelope was finally opened, the whole thing tore in two.

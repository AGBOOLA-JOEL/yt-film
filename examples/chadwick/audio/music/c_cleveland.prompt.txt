Instrumental only, no vocals, no choir. An underscore that sits under a spoken narration: plenty of space, no sudden loud hits, steady dynamics. About three minutes, ending with a natural fade. Cleveland in the 1880s and 1890s: a fortune teller's candlelit parlour, false names, short marriages, prison and parole, a wealthy avenue of mansions that snubs her. Sly, playful-dark chamber underscore, around 80 BPM: a slow waltz on piano and clarinet, pizzicato strings, a soft bassoon; theatrical and a little sinister.

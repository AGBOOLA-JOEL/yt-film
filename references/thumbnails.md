# Thumbnails

Made in an image tool (ChatGPT, Flow, Midjourney...) from a short guide Claude writes after the final render:
`<project>/THUMBNAIL_GUIDE.md` with the opener below, four variant prompts, the reference images to attach and title
ideas. Save results as `<project>/thumbnails/thumb_A.png` ... `thumb_D.png`.

## What works
- **One big face doing something**, filling about half the frame: a finger to the lips, an object held to the camera,
  a look over glasses. With egg-headed characters the expression is the mouth line, head tilt, eyes' direction,
  hands and light. Use your film's own characters (attach the style card and their cast sheets), very close up.
- **Movie-poster light** on your art style: saturated colour, warm/cold rim light, deep shadow, a clear foreground.
- **A graphic device that tells the story**: a crack through a face, a red arrow, a split light, ghost outlines.
- **2–3 huge words** in heavy condensed capitals (white/yellow with thick black outline) on the dark side, or none.
- **A question in the viewer's head** that the title doesn't answer.
- Readable at phone size; the bottom-right corner clear (the timestamp sits there).

Not: a still from the film with words on top, small faces, flat light, busy layouts, clickbait the film doesn't pay off.

## Opener (adapt the style line to your art style)
```
We're making YouTube thumbnails for a documentary channel. The attached style card and character sheets show how
every person must look: [CHARACTER STYLE, e.g. an egg-shaped head, plain cream skin, two small dark oval eyes, one
thin line for the mouth, no nose, ears or eyebrows], matching their sheet exactly, painted in the same [ART STYLE],
but lit like a movie poster. For every image:
- Rich colour, dramatic warm and cold rim light, glow, deep shadows behind.
- ONE dominant subject: a very close character whose head and hands fill about half the frame, doing something.
- A graphic device that tells the story when asked.
- Huge headline words in heavy condensed capitals, white and bright yellow with thick black outlines, on the darker
  side, perfectly spelled. No other text.
- 16:9, built to read at phone size; bottom-right corner clear. No logos, no watermarks, no gore.
Reply "ready".
```
Then one variant per message, e.g. *"CASSIE, very close, holding a sealed envelope up to the camera with a sly half
smile, a crack of red light down the envelope; words: 'SHE FAKED IT'."*

If the face isn't the first thing you see at phone size: *"Same idea, push in: the face twice as big, background
darker, stronger rim light."* Words badly spelled? Ask for a no-text version and add the words yourself (Canva,
Photopea, or ask Claude to set them with Pillow in the same style).

Test two or three with YouTube's thumbnail test (Studio → Test & Compare) on upload.
